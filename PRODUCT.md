# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Primary: UK small-business owners (removals firms, property buyers, trades, local services) whose jobs, leads and invoices live in spreadsheets, inboxes and memory, or whose existing app needs fixing or looking after. They are not technical, arrive from a link or search, and decide in seconds whether this person can solve their problem and what it costs.

Secondary: agencies, founders and hiring managers who want proof of engineering depth one click below the surface.

## Product Purpose
alihasansheikh.com is the sales page for Ali Hasan Sheikh, a solo software developer in Karachi working UK hours. Success is a visitor sending a message through the contact form (or email/WhatsApp) about a real project.

## Positioning
One developer, not an agency, who has already built and still maintains two production systems that UK companies run on daily, leads with custom systems (CRMs, client portals, job systems), agrees a fixed price before work starts, and hands over every login and line of code. UK first; businesses anywhere in English welcome.

## Operating Context
Visitors compare him with Upwork freelancers and small agencies. Four services: CRMs, client portals and job systems; automation and AI assistants; business websites; fixes, features and ongoing care. No prices are shown anywhere; every job gets a fixed quote before work starts. White-label work for agencies is offered too.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Next.js 15 App Router, React 19, Tailwind 4, TypeScript, hosted on Vercel Hobby. Page should stay static; contact form is a Server Action sending through Resend.
- All surface copy lives in `data/copy.ts` and was approved by the owner; British spelling, plain English, sentences under 25 words, no buzzwords, no em-dashes.
- Process promises the site makes: reply within one working day; online for the whole UK working day; fixed price before work starts; progress updates in UK hours; client owns code, hosting and accounts; free fixes for 14 days after handover.

## Brand Commitments
- Owner request (Nov 2026): an attractive, trendy site with animation, built with shadcn/ui, Magic UI and React Bits components where they earn their place, and explicitly not AI slop.
- Client names are never shown; case studies are anonymous ("Removals company, UK", "Property-buying business, UK"). The employer is left out. No Upwork link until there are reviews.
- Every contact route points to alihasansheikh01@gmail.com for now.

## Evidence on Hand
- Case studies: the removals system, the property lead pipeline, BuilderConnect (university project, must be labelled as such). Facts in `data/copy.ts` and `data/projects.ts`.
- Photo: `public/ali.jpg` (placeholder selfie; a better photo is pending from the owner).
- CV: `public/cv.pdf` (still names the clients and employer; owner to replace).
- No testimonials, client logos, screenshots or metrics beyond those in the code. Do not invent any.

## Product Principles
- A non-technical owner understands what he does, for whom, and what to do next within five seconds.
- Nothing invented: every claim traces to the code or to a promise he keeps.
- Engineering depth is available but never the loudest thing on the page.
- The contact action is always one move away.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Minimum text 12px, link targets at least 24px tall and buttons 44px, visible keyboard focus, motion behind `prefers-reduced-motion`, WCAG AA contrast.
